About Us

Established in 2022, Selelo Business Park was founded with a bold vision: to create a self-sustaining city within a city. Strategically located along the Namanga Highway, we integrate real estate, logistics, and commercial opportunities into one thriving hub.

Unlike typical developments, Selelo Business Park is fully owned by its proprietor — not leased — ensuring long-term value, security, and stability for all investors and stakeholders.

Vision

To become East Africa’s premier mixed-use business destination, combining real estate, logistics, and commerce into a sustainable urban ecosystem.

Mission

To deliver world-class infrastructure, innovative business spaces, and integrated solutions that foster growth, connectivity, and long-term value for our partners and community.
